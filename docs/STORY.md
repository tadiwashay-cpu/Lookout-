# LOOKOUT — Story Script

> *"Good and bad are just what people call the weather. I go out in it either way."* — Nash

---

## 1. Tone

**Grounded street drama with a superhuman engine.** Think a cold harbour night:
sodium streetlights, wet asphalt, engines idling. The city of **Halden Bay** is
not evil. It's tired, and everyone in it is negotiating.

- **Nash is deadpan.** He rarely raises his voice. His humour is dry, and his
  face gives nothing away; the half-lidded stare from his design *is* the
  character. When he finally shows anger, it lands because he hardly ever does.
- **No lectures.** The game never tells the player what is right. Every choice
  costs something on both sides. Helping the police makes enemies on the
  street; robbing a bank feeds a family. The camera doesn't judge, and neither
  does the script.
- **Violence is weight, not gore.** Fights are loud, physical, and over fast.
  The Def Jam camera and slow-motion finishers make them feel big; blood stays
  off-screen.
- **Rhythm:** quiet, tense conversations, then sudden bursts of speed. Every
  act ends with a race or a fight that answers the conversation that came
  before it.
- **Music direction:** low, heavy beats on foot; driving synth-trap in the car;
  near silence (wind, the harbour, a distant siren) in the big emotional scenes.
- **Visual language:** daytime is washed out and ordinary. Night is when the
  story happens. Project Axiom is always **cold blue light**, the same blue as
  Nash's surge.

**Touchstones:** *Def Jam: Fight for NY* (crews, the ring, respect),
*NFS: Most Wanted* (the city as a racing ladder), *Chronicle* and *Brightburn*
(powers as a problem, not a gift), *Training Day* (a cop you can't trust).

## 2. Characters

| Character | Who they are | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| **Nash** | 24. Grew up in the harbour flats without a father. A hustler: robs a bank one week, sells a crew to the police the next. Believes in nothing but the next move. After Axiom: inhumanly fast and strong, with a power surge, and he learns any fighting style after seeing it once. | Few words, flat delivery, sharp when it counts. |
| **Maxim Varro** | Nash's father. Owns **Varro Freight** on the docks: legitimate shipping on paper, smuggling underneath. Charming, patient, cruel. Left Nash's mother when Nash was four and never looked back. Behind Project Axiom's local shipment. | Warm on the surface, ice underneath. Never swears. Calls people "son" when he's threatening them. |
| **Deacon "Deke" Hollis** | Leader of the **Saint Ames Boys**, the crew that jumped Nash on the night of the vials. Ambitious, loud, does freelance muscle for Varro Freight. | Big talk, big laugh, scared underneath. |
| **Imani "Mani" Cole** | Nash's oldest friend. Runs the **Auto Repair** on Ninth. Fixes cars, launders a little, keeps Nash alive. The closest thing he has to a conscience, though she'd hate being called that. | Fast, funny, honest to the point of rude. |
| **Detective Ruth Okafor** | 84th Precinct. Uses Nash as an informant. Wants Varro badly, and isn't above using a superhuman she doesn't control to get him. | Calm, tired, always negotiating. |
| **Dr. Lena Sato** | Scientist who worked on Project Axiom before it was sold to private buyers. Knows what is happening to Nash's body. | Precise, guilt-heavy, speaks in facts. |
| **Kade** | Maxim's enforcer. Ex-military, injected with an earlier Axiom formula. Nash's mirror: power without any doubt. | Almost silent. When he talks, it's orders. |
| **Coach Bram** | Runs the **Iron Gym** and the **Clean Hands** ring in the harbour yard. Bare knuckles only: no weapons, no exceptions. | Old-school, gravel voice, respects only fists. |
| **Tavi** | Street-race organiser. Runs the **Grand Loop**: downtown, the harbour, over the pass, through the suburbs and the forest. | Hyper, talks in hype, always filming. |

## 3. The choice system: *The Ledger*

Every major choice moves Nash on two hidden meters, **Street** and **Badge**.
They are not good and evil; they measure who trusts him.

- **Street high:** crews back him, races pay more, and the police hunt harder.
- **Badge high:** Okafor clears his heat and gives him intel; crews ambush him.
- **Both balanced:** nobody fully trusts him. This unlocks the hidden ending.

The HUD never shows these meters. Characters react to them in dialogue.

## 4. Script

Format: **CHARACTER:** line. *(Stage directions in italics.)* `[CHOICE]` marks
a player decision; `[GAMEPLAY]` marks what the player does.

### PROLOGUE — "The Vials"

**SCENE 0.1 — Harbour, night. Varro Freight loading yard.**

*(Rain. Nash leans on a fence across from the Varro Freight gate, hood up. A
phone buzzes.)*

**MANI** *(on the phone)*: Tell me you're not where I think you are.

**NASH:** I'm looking for work.

**MANI:** At your *father's* place.

**NASH:** He's got money. I need money. It's not a reunion.

**MANI:** Nash. Walk away.

**NASH:** I'll call you back.

*(He hangs up. Inside the yard, men unload a truck. The crates are stencilled
**PROJECT AXIOM — BIOHAZARD — DO NOT OPEN**. One crate is cracked open; inside,
rows of glass vials glow a cold blue.)*

`[GAMEPLAY]` Sneak through the yard (tutorial: movement, crouching behind
containers, camera).

*(A guard on the radio walks past Nash's hiding spot.)*

**GUARD** *(into the radio)*: Mr. Varro says nobody touches the blue crate.
Not even us. The buyer's flying in Thursday.

*(Nash watches him go. He looks at the vials. A long beat.)*

**NASH** *(to himself)*: Thursday's a long way off.

*(He pockets three vials. An alarm trips. Floodlights.)*

`[GAMEPLAY]` Chase on foot: escape the yard through the container stacks.

---

**SCENE 0.2 — An alley off the harbour. Moments later.**

*(Nash slows to a walk, breathing hard, and almost laughs. Then headlights.
Five men step out: the Saint Ames Boys. DEKE in front.)*

**DEKE:** Nash! Running from Varro's yard at midnight. Funny thing: Varro pays
us to watch that yard.

**NASH:** Then you're bad at it.

**DEKE:** *(laughs)* See, that mouth. Empty your pockets.

**NASH:** No.

**DEKE:** Wasn't a question.

`[GAMEPLAY]` Unwinnable fight (tutorial: punch, block, dodge). Nash lands a few
hits before he's overwhelmed.

*(They beat him down. Deke kneels, finds one vial, and holds it up to the light.)*

**DEKE:** Pretty. Varro'll pay me twice for this.

*(He shoves Nash's face into the ground. The two vials still in Nash's jacket
crack under his weight. Blue light seeps through the fabric into his skin.
Nash convulses. The Saint Ames Boys back away.)*

**SAINT AMES BOY:** Deke... Deke, what's he doing?

**DEKE:** Leave him. Leave him!

*(They run. Nash lies alone in the rain. The blue light pulses in his veins,
then goes dark. Silence.)*

**TITLE CARD: L O O K O U T**

---

### ACT 1 — "Fallout"

**SCENE 1.1 — Mani's Auto Repair. Morning.**

*(Nash wakes on the back seat of a car on the lift. Mani is sitting on a tool
chest, watching him.)*

**MANI:** You were in a puddle by the fish market. I thought you were dead.

**NASH:** Disappointed?

**MANI:** Relieved. Then I saw your arms.

*(Faint blue lines under his skin fade as he looks.)*

**NASH:** It's nothing.

**MANI:** Your *veins* were glowing, Nash. That's not nothing. That's a medical
event.

*(Nash stands. The car on the lift rocks as he puts a hand on it to steady
himself, and the steel frame of the lift bends. They both stare at it.)*

**MANI:** ...You're paying for that.

`[GAMEPLAY]` Tutorial: Nash's new strength and speed. Sprint across the
workshop and out into the city at superhuman speed.

---

**SCENE 1.2 — Rooftop, downtown. Day.**

*(Nash sits on a roof edge. His phone rings: DETECTIVE OKAFOR.)*

**OKAFOR:** Varro's yard had a break-in last night. Three vials of something
expensive are gone. You wouldn't know anything about that.

**NASH:** I know lots of things.

**OKAFOR:** The Saint Ames Boys are telling people you have them. Varro is
going to come asking. I'd like to ask first.

**NASH:** What's in it for me?

**OKAFOR:** I keep you alive long enough to find out what you took.

`[CHOICE]`
- **"I'll find out what it is. Then we talk."** *(Badge +1)*
- **"Lose my number."** *(Street +1)*

---

**MISSION 1 — "Saint Ames"** *(revenge, part 1)*

*(Night. The Saint Ames Boys' block in the suburbs. Nash walks up the middle
of the street. Porch lights come on.)*

**SAINT AMES BOY:** Yo... that's him. That's the dead guy.

**DEKE** *(from a porch)*: Nash! You walked here? After last night?

**NASH:** You took something of mine.

**DEKE:** I took something of *Varro's*. Which makes you a thief who got
robbed. Go home.

**NASH:** Where's the vial, Deke?

**DEKE:** *(beat)* Sold it. Who do you think? He paid. Very well.

`[GAMEPLAY]` Street brawl against six Saint Ames Boys. Def Jam camera, first
finisher tutorial. Deke escapes in a car → chase through the suburbs.

*(Deke's car wrecks against a garage. Nash pulls him out by the collar.)*

**DEKE:** Okay! Okay! Varro paid me five grand and asked what you looked like.
That's it, I swear.

**NASH:** He asked what I looked like.

**DEKE:** Like he didn't already know! That's your old man, isn't it? Everyone
knows. Everyone but you, apparently.

`[CHOICE]`
- **Let him go.** *(Street +1. Deke owes you; he shows up again in Act 3.)*
- **Hand him to Okafor.** *(Badge +2. The Saint Ames Boys become hostile across
  the suburbs.)*
- **Finisher.** *(Street −1, Badge −1. Deke ends up in hospital and out of the
  story. Nash's next scene with Mani is colder.)*

---

**SCENE 1.3 — The Harbor Diner. Night.**

*(A booth. Dr. LENA SATO sits across from Nash, untouched coffee in front of
her. Okafor set up the meeting.)*

**SATO:** May I see your hand?

*(She presses two fingers to his wrist and frowns at her watch.)*

**SATO:** Your resting heart rate is thirty-one. You should be unconscious.

**NASH:** I feel fine.

**SATO:** Axiom doesn't make you strong, Mr. Nash. It rebuilds you, cell by
cell, around whatever you were doing when it entered your body. You were
fighting. So now your body is built for fighting.

**NASH:** And the side effects?

**SATO:** The test subjects who took the earlier formula became very calm, very
fast and very obedient. Then they stopped sleeping. Then they stopped caring
about anything except the next order.

**NASH:** I already don't care about much.

**SATO:** *(quietly)* That's what worries me.

---

### ACT 2 — "The Ladder"

*Maxim never appears in person. To get to him, Nash has to climb the city: win
the races that Varro bets on, and fight in the ring where Varro recruits.*

**SCENE 2.1 — The harbour yard. Clean Hands ring.**

*(A crowd around the ring. Floodlights. COACH BRAM at the ropes.)*

**BRAM:** Rules are in the name, son. No weapons, no tricks. Hands only. You
win, you eat. You lose, you go home in somebody's trunk.

**NASH:** Varro watches these fights.

**BRAM:** Varro *owns* these fights. Every champion here ends up working his
door. You want to meet the man? Become worth meeting.

`[GAMEPLAY]` **Clean Hands career:** five fight nights against escalating
fighters. After the third, Bram notices Nash copying every opponent's style.

**BRAM:** You threw that man's own hook back at him. Where'd you learn that?

**NASH:** Just now.

**BRAM:** *(long look)* Don't let them see that. Men who learn that fast end up
owned.

---

**SCENE 2.2 — Downtown, midnight. Tavi's meet.**

*(Thirty cars idling under the overpass. TAVI is filming everything.)*

**TAVI:** Nash! The man, the myth, the guy who walked away from a Saint Ames
beating! Okay, okay. You want in on the Loop?

**NASH:** I want the Varro Freight money that's riding on it.

**TAVI:** Everybody does, baby. Varro's driver has won the Loop nine weeks
straight. Black car, no plates, drives like a ghost.

**NASH:** Who is he?

**TAVI:** Nobody knows. He never gets out of the car.

`[GAMEPLAY]` **Street Race career:** four races building up to the Grand Loop.
The ghost driver is **Kade**.

---

**MISSION 6 — "First Harbor"** *(the bank)*

*(Okafor and Mani both call within a minute of each other.)*

**OKAFOR:** Varro launders his Axiom money through First Harbor Bank, on
Thursday nights. Get me the ledger from the vault and I can put him away.

**MANI:** The Saint Ames Boys are hitting First Harbor *tonight*. Varro's money
is in that vault. If you go in with them you clear your debts, my debts, the
whole block's debts.

**NASH:** You both want me in the same bank.

**MANI:** For different reasons.

**NASH:** Story of my life.

`[CHOICE]` *(changes the whole mission)*
- **Rob it with the crew:** take the cash, burn the ledger. *(Street +2)*
- **Take the ledger for Okafor:** stop the robbery from the inside. *(Badge +2)*
- **Take both:** cash for the block, ledger for Okafor, tell nobody.
  *(Street +1, Badge +1. Unlocks the hidden-ending path. Much harder.)*

`[GAMEPLAY]` Heist inside the bank interior, then a police chase with three
stars and an escape over the harbour.

---

**SCENE 2.3 — The Grand Loop. Night.**

*(Final race. Nash's car on the line beside the black car. The window rolls
down an inch: KADE, blue light behind his eyes.)*

**KADE:** You're the son.

**NASH:** I'm a guy in a car.

**KADE:** He said you'd say that.

`[GAMEPLAY]` The Grand Loop against Kade. Kade uses a car surge. When Nash
wins, Kade rams him off the mountain pass on purpose.

*(Nash's car tumbles down the slope. The surge flares and he walks out of the
wreck. Kade is waiting at the bottom, unhurt.)*

**KADE:** Mr. Varro would like to see you.

**NASH:** Then he can come down here.

**KADE:** He thought you'd say that too.

`[GAMEPLAY]` First boss fight against Kade, who can't be beaten yet. Kade
leaves Nash on his knees.

**KADE:** You're fast. You think. That's the problem. I don't.

---

### ACT 3 — "Axiom"

**SCENE 3.1 — Varro Freight, Maxim's office above the docks.**

*(Nash walks in alone. MAXIM VARRO stands at the window with his back turned.
The harbour lights are reflected in the glass.)*

**MAXIM:** You have your mother's way of entering a room. As if it owes you
money.

**NASH:** It does.

**MAXIM:** *(turning)* Look at you. Twenty-four years. And the first thing you
ever take from me is the only thing in this city worth stealing. That's almost
flattering.

**NASH:** You knew it was me. At the yard.

**MAXIM:** I knew the second the alarm went off. I could have had you shot. I
had you watched instead. Do you know why?

**NASH:** Because you're a coward.

**MAXIM:** Because you're *proof*, son. The original formula gave me soldiers
like Kade: obedient, empty. You took it raw, in a fight, angry. And you can
still *choose*. Do you know what a buyer pays for that?

**NASH:** You're selling *me*.

**MAXIM:** I'm offering you a place. For the first time in your life. Stand
next to me Thursday when the buyer lands, and you'll never be hungry again.

*(Long silence.)*

**NASH:** Where were you? My whole life.

**MAXIM:** *(without hesitation)* Busy.

`[CHOICE]`
- **"Thursday, then."** Pretend to join him. *(Street +1; sets up the double cross.)*
- **Attack him now.** Kade steps in and Nash is thrown out of the window into
  the harbour. *(Badge +1; harder Act 3.)*

---

**SCENE 3.2 — Mani's workshop. Night.**

*(Nash sits on the bonnet of the car on the lift, the one whose frame he bent.
Mani works on it beneath him.)*

**MANI:** So he offered you a job.

**NASH:** A family business.

**MANI:** And?

**NASH:** And for about four seconds I wanted it. *(beat)* That's the part I
don't like.

**MANI:** *(sliding out from under the car)* You know what your problem is? You
think not caring makes you free. It just makes you easy to point.

**NASH:** Sato said the same thing. With more syllables.

**MANI:** Smart woman. *(wiping her hands)* Whatever you do Thursday, do it
because *you* chose it. Not him, not Okafor, not me.

---

**MISSION 12 — "Thursday"** *(finale)*

*(The harbour at night. A cargo plane lands on the coast highway, which has
been closed off. Maxim waits with Kade and twenty men. Okafor's units hide in
the container stacks. On the rooftops, the Saint Ames Boys, if Deke was spared.)*

**OKAFOR** *(in Nash's earpiece)*: When the buyer opens the case, we move. Not
before.

**MAXIM:** *(to Nash, arms wide)* My son, gentlemen. Proof of concept.

**BUYER:** He's the one who took it in a fight?

**MAXIM:** Show them, Nash.

*(Every eye on Nash.)*

`[CHOICE — the ending branches]`

**A. "Street" ending — Nash takes the case.**
*(Nash surges and flattens everyone, cops included, and takes the Axiom case.)*

**OKAFOR:** Nash! Nash, don't you—

**NASH:** You used me. He used me. My turn.

`[GAMEPLAY]` Fight Kade on the runway and escape across the whole map with
every faction chasing you. Final shot: Nash on the mountain pass at dawn, the
case on the passenger seat, glowing blue. He owns the city's black market now.

**NASH** *(voice-over)*: Everybody in Halden Bay wanted to own me. So I bought
the store.

---

**B. "Badge" ending — Nash brings Maxim in.**

**NASH:** *(into the earpiece)* Now.

*(Floodlights. Police everywhere. Maxim doesn't even look surprised.)*

**MAXIM:** The police. Really? After everything they've done to people like us?

**NASH:** You did more.

`[GAMEPLAY]` Final fight with Kade beside the plane. Finisher: Nash overloads
Kade's surge, and the blue drains out of his eyes.

**KADE:** *(quietly, human for the first time)* ...Thank you.

*(Maxim is handcuffed. He leans close as he passes Nash.)*

**MAXIM:** They'll want you next, son. You know that.

**NASH:** Let them come.

*(Final shot: Okafor hands Nash a badge-shaped informant card. He pockets it
without looking. Ambiguous.)*

---

**C. Hidden ending, "Lookout" (Street and Badge balanced, and you took both
at the bank)**

*(Nash opens the case himself and empties every vial onto the runway. They
shatter, and the blue light sinks into the wet concrete and fades.)*

**MAXIM:** *(for the first time, real anger)* Do you know what you've just—

**NASH:** Yeah. Nobody gets to be me.

*(Okafor's units move in. The Saint Ames Boys block the buyer's escape. Mani's
tow truck blocks the runway. Every side Nash ever played shows up at once.)*

`[GAMEPLAY]` Fight Kade with all factions fighting beside you, then chase
Maxim's car across the whole map to the forest.

*(Maxim's car is wrecked in the trees. Nash pulls him out and holds him by
the collar, his fist raised and surging blue. Long beat.)*

**MAXIM:** Go on. Prove you're mine.

*(Nash lowers his fist. The blue fades.)*

**NASH:** I'm not.

*(He walks away. Sirens arrive behind him.)*

**Final scene, rooftop, dawn.** *(Nash and Mani sit on the roof edge from
Scene 1.2.)*

**MANI:** So what are you now? Hero? Villain?

**NASH:** Lookout.

**MANI:** ...What does that even mean?

**NASH:** Somebody's got to watch this city. Doesn't mean I have to like it.

*(He stands, steps off the roof, and lands in the street. The camera pulls up
over Halden Bay.)*

**END**
