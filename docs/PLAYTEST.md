# Lookout playtest guide (v0.3.0)

For everyone testing at school. Ten minutes each is enough.

## Install
1. Get `lookout-v0.3.0.apk` (GitHub > Releases > "Lookout v0.3.0 (playtest)",
   or have it sent to you).
2. Tap the file, allow "install unknown apps", open **Lookout**.
3. Settings > GRAPHICS: leave it on HIGH for the first run. Turn the FPS
   counter ON.

## What to play (in this order)
| # | Do this | Look for |
|---|---------|----------|
| 1 | FREE ROAM, 2 minutes on foot: sprint, jump, punch the street hustlers | Does Nash feel fast and heavy? Does the camera feel right? |
| 2 | Get in a car (ENTER), drive the highway, drift, use nitro, hit traffic | Handling, sense of speed, anything invisible blocking the road |
| 3 | Tap the radar, find AUTO REPAIR, walk inside a building | Map clear? Doors work? |
| 4 | CLEAN HANDS solo, 2 minutes, MEDIUM | Do hits land when they should? Finisher? Is it fun? |
| 5 | STREET RACE solo, 1 lap, 5 bots | Bots fair? Grenades/surge useful? |
| 6 | Two or more devices on one Wi-Fi/hotspot: one HOSTS, others JOIN | Does everyone connect? Lag? Do KOs and positions match on all screens? |

## Send back
Menu > **PLAYTEST REPORT** > **COPY REPORT**, paste it, then add:

```
Tester:
Device (if the report got it wrong):

FEEL (1 bad - 5 great)
  Running/jumping:      Fighting:      Driving:      Camera:

SMOOTHNESS
  Where did it stutter or drop below 60?
  Did it get hot or drain the battery fast?

BUGS (what you did -> what happened)
  1.
  2.

FUN
  Best moment:
  Most annoying thing:
  One thing to add next:
```

Screenshots help: the version is printed in the bottom-left corner of every
one.

## Known gaps in 0.3.0
- Story missions are not in yet (script: docs/STORY.md).
- Nash and the street people are the old models; new ones are being made.
- Tyre squeal and the crowd are still synthesised sounds.
- Traffic only drives the main loop and doesn't use indicators or lights.
- Online play needs everyone on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot.
